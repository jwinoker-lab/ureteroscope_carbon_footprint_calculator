Outcome-adjusted LCA · flexible ureteroscopy

Does your single-use ureteroscope have a lower carbon footprint per care episode compared to your reusable option?

The answer depends heavily on your hospital's electricity and on how often ureteral access fails with each scope. Choose your practice location and enter your estimated primary ureteroscopy failure rates; everything else starts at a published or estimated default you can change under Advanced inputs.

BWhat one failed access adds

A failed access means a pre-stent and a return to the OR. Only activities caused by the failure count. The pre-stent comes out at the return visit, and a post-treatment stent occurs either way, so neither adds a removal. The return visit's scope is counted in A.

    How to use this calculator, with a worked example
    1. Where is your hospital? Pick your country, and your state in the U.S. If your hospital buys renewable electricity, you can choose the renewable-contract option, but report the location-based result too.
    2. How often does access fail? Enter the share of unstented patients in whom access fails with each scope, from your own audit if you have one.
    3. Read the result. The sentence at the top gives the verdict. The bar chart shows how much comes from the scopes and how much from the extra care after failed access. The list under it says which values would flip the answer.
    4. Refine if you have data. Advanced inputs hold the scope inventories and the cost of one failed access. Save your inputs and results as a CSV or a link.

    Worked example

    An Ohio hospital (eGRID subregion RFCW, 0.479 kg/kWh) uses Thöne's reprocessing inventory and audits failed access at 8% with its reusable scope and 3% with a 6.3 Fr single-use scope. Every other input stays at its default.

      Formulas and sources
      e_RU = M_RU/L + (kWh_rep × CI + C_rep)/n_cyc + R/N_r + u_RU F = OR_fixed + s × (OR_time + kWh_OR × CI + gases) + stent + p_ED × E_ED + p_adm × LOS × E_day [+ travel] OR_time = OR energy + anesthesia drugs and IV fluids; OR_fixed = the other return-to-OR components s = share of OR time and anesthesia used by the aborted first attempt F* = [e_SU(1 + p_SU) − e_RU(1 + p_RU)] / (p_RU − p_SU) single-use lower when F > F* p_RU* = [e_SU − e_RU + p_SU(e_SU + F)] / (e_RU + F) e_SU* = [e_RU(1 + p_RU) + (p_RU − p_SU)F] / (1 + p_SU) single-use lower when e_SU < e_SU* Other tipping points: the model is linear in each input, so each is found by solving for the value where the two strategies tie. Uncertainty: each input ~ triangular(low, current, high), independent, 5,000 draws, seed 2026
      • Grid intensities: U.S. national average and states from EPA eGRID2023 at the subregion level (each state assigned its primary subregion; New York split into Upstate, Downstate and Long Island). Other countries: national averages compiled by the study team.
      • Device defaults: Thöne M et al. Urolithiasis 2024;52:166. Davis NF et al. J Endourol 2018;32:214–217. Merlin P et al. J Endourol 2025;39:1230–1237. The Davis inventory is entered from its Table 2: reusable manufacture 0.06 and solid waste 0.005 kg per use over 180 uses (11.7 kg per scope), 7.89 kWh per reprocessing cycle shared by 2 scopes, repackaging 0.01 kg per cycle, repairs 0.45 kg per use (7.2 kg every 16 uses), and LithoVue 4.43 kg per use. At Davis's grid of about 1.0 kg/kWh this reproduces its 4.47 kg reusable total.
      • Location: the calculator has no default grid, because the reprocessing footprint depends on it more than on anything else. The renewable-contract option is market-based accounting: it counts purchased renewable electricity as zero. The GHG Protocol asks for the location-based value to be reported alongside it.
      • Single-use range: published totals run from 2.28 kg (LithoVue, Merlin, manufacturing processes excluded) to 6.25 kg (Thöne, conventional German grid at the hospital). Thöne does not report which stages rise on the conventional grid. The uncertainty analysis uses this span as the single-use range.
      • Access failure: Morgan K et al. Urolithiasis 2023;51:41 (15.4%); Viers BR et al. Urology 2015;86:878–884 (16%); Khan HS et al. PAFMJ 2018 (7.9%); Cetti RJ et al. Ann R Coll Surg Engl 2011 (about 8%); Ebner B et al. Diagnostics 2025 (6.3 Fr, 5.3%).
      • Return to the OR: a failed access adds one aborted attempt. The model counts the return-to-OR session as a complete case and credits back the OR time and anesthesia that the short aborted attempt did not use. Drapes, gowns, disposables, linen and tray reprocessing, recovery, waste and the pre-stent are used in full either way, so the aborted-attempt share does not scale them. Patient travel, when included, also counts in full.
      • OR session components: John JB et al. BJU Int 2024;134:268–281 (day-case transurethral resection, 121 kg median including patient and staff travel). Range: Merlin implies about 21 kg per ureteroscopy without OR ventilation; MacNeill AJ et al. Lancet Planet Health 2017, 146 to 232 kg per operation across a mixed case mix.
      • Anesthetic gases: ASA car-mile equivalents per MAC-hour at 1 L/min (sevoflurane 4, isoflurane 8) × 0.403 kg per mile. Scales roughly with time and fresh gas flow. Desflurane and nitrous oxide are excluded: both are being phased out, and leaving them out lowers the cost of a failed access, which works against single-use.
      • ED visits and admissions: Elliott CS et al. Urology 2018;117:44–49 (6.6% ED, 2.2% admission at 7 days); Michigan ROCKS (about 10% ED, 3.9% admission at 30 days); Carlos EC et al. J Urol 2019 (12.6% ED); Washington University (14.6% ED, 5.1% admission).
      • Inpatient day 45 kg: Thiel CL et al. Int J LCA 2022. Repeat imaging before the second operation is excluded because it is not routine. Vehicle 0.4 kg per mile: US EPA. The ED visit value is an assumption; no validated ED-visit LCA was found.
      • Uncertainty: inputs are drawn independently, so it ignores correlations (for example, a hospital with a long OR session may also have longer anesthetic times). Default ranges are placeholders: OR session 24 to 170 kg; ED visits 6.6% to 14.6%; admissions 2.2% to 5.1%; single-use footprint 2.28 to 6.25 kg; others scaled from the current value. Replace them with your own.
