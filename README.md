Outcomes-adjusted carbon footprint calculator for single-use vs reusable flexible ureteroscopes

The question of 'which is more environmentally sustainable?" is not a one-size-fits-all equation. Numerous factors need to be considered, such as the reusable scope lifecycle and utilization, the reprocessing footprint (including consumables required and utility draw per reprocessing cycle), and macro infrastructure (including end-of-life processes and carbon intensity of the local energy grid).
However, what is often overlooked is the impact of scope advances in single-use technology. For example, the growing availability of smaller caliber ureteroscopes may result in fewer cases of failed ureteral access in unstented patients, and therefore fewer secondary procedures and their associated footprints. 

This calculator allows users to compare the calculated carbon footprints of their single-use and reusable ureteroscope option on not only a per-case (per-scope) basis, but on a per-care episode basis (which accounts for lower failed access with smaller caliber scopes). 

Where users do not know inputs to their reusable ureteroscope utilization factors, or the carbon footprint of their single-use scope, we have provided default values based on currently available published Life Cycle Assessment figures (as of October 1, 2026).
